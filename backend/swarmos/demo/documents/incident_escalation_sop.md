# Incident Escalation — Sample Internal Procedure

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT generated for SwarmOS demonstrations. Internal classification example.

## Severity levels
Low: inconvenience with no safety impact. Moderate: localised service disruption. High: safety risk or a large affected population. Critical: imminent threat to life or critical infrastructure.

## Acknowledgement targets
Critical incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes, high within 30 minutes, moderate within 4 hours and low within 24 hours.

## Escalation
Unacknowledged critical incidents escalate to the duty officer. Incidents involving hospitals, water treatment plants or substations are always treated as at least high severity.

## Human authorisation
Automated systems, including AI agents, may recommend escalations but must not change incident status, dispatch crews or publish public messages without an authorised operator's approval.
