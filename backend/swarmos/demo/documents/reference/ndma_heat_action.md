# Heat Action Planning — Reference Summary of NDMA Guidance

> Summary written by SwarmOS for orientation. It is not an official document; see NDMA's Guidelines for Preparation of Action Plan – Prevention and Management of Heat Wave (2019 revision) at https://ndma.gov.in and your city's own Heat Action Plan.

## Heat action plans
NDMA advises states and cities to prepare heat action plans that define local temperature thresholds, an early warning system, inter-agency coordination, public awareness, capacity building of health workers and long-term measures such as cool roofs and urban greening.

## Before and during a heat wave
Common measures include issuing colour-coded alerts to departments and the public; ensuring drinking water at bus stands, markets and work sites; opening cooling shelters; rescheduling outdoor work, school hours and public events away from the hottest hours (roughly noon to 4 pm); keeping ORS and IV fluids stocked; and preparing hospitals to recognise and treat heat illness.

## Vulnerable groups
Outdoor workers, older adults, infants and young children, pregnant women, people with chronic disease, and people without shelter are at the greatest risk. Outreach should reach them directly, not only through media advisories.

## Monitoring and review
Cities should track heat-related illness and deaths during the season and review what worked after it, adjusting thresholds and measures in the next year's plan.
