# Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 — Reference Summary

> Summary written by SwarmOS for orientation. It is not the legal text; see the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change notification of the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 for the authoritative provisions.

## Segregation at source
Every waste generator must segregate waste into three streams: biodegradable (wet), non-biodegradable (dry), and domestic hazardous waste such as used batteries, paint and medicines, and hand them to authorised collectors. Sanitary waste should be securely wrapped.

## Duties of urban local bodies
Local bodies are responsible for door-to-door collection of segregated waste, providing separate storage and transport for each stream, setting up material recovery facilities, promoting composting and bio-methanation for wet waste, and sending only residual inert waste to sanitary landfills. They may levy user fees and spot fines through bye-laws.

## Bulk generators and prohibitions
Bulk generators (for example large housing societies, markets, hotels and institutions above the notified size) must segregate and process wet waste on site where feasible. Littering, burning of waste and burying it in the open are prohibited.

## Operational signals
Repeated garbage complaints from the same ward, overflowing secondary collection points and open burning reports are typical indicators that collection frequency or segregation compliance needs attention.
