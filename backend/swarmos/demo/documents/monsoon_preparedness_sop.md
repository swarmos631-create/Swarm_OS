# Monsoon Preparedness — Sample Standard Operating Procedure

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT generated for SwarmOS demonstrations. It is not an official policy of any city or agency.

## Purpose
Reduce disruption from heavy rainfall and urban flooding by preparing drains, pumps and response teams before and during the monsoon.

## Pre-monsoon (April–May)
Complete desilting of primary and secondary storm water drains. Inspect culverts at known waterlogging points. Test every dewatering pump and record its location. Update the list of low-lying wards and underpasses.

## Trigger levels
When the forecast for the next 24 hours exceeds 64.5 mm (heavy rainfall), the duty officer alerts ward engineers and places dewatering crews on standby. When the forecast exceeds 115.6 mm (very heavy rainfall), pumps are pre-positioned at known waterlogging points and traffic police are informed of likely diversions.

## During heavy rain
Monitor drainage complaints by ward. Prioritise underpasses, hospital access roads and arterial roads. Any incident involving a fallen tree on a live wire is escalated to the electricity utility immediately.

## Approval
Pre-positioning of pumps and diversion plans require approval by the duty officer. Closure of arterial roads requires approval by the traffic police.
