# Heat Action — Sample Operational Checklist

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT generated for SwarmOS demonstrations. It is not an official heat action plan.

## Activation
Activate heat measures when the forecast maximum temperature reaches 45 °C, or when the duty officer judges that humidity makes conditions dangerous at lower temperatures.

## Measures
Open designated cooling centres and publish their locations. Ensure drinking water points at bus stations and markets. Reschedule outdoor municipal work away from 12:00–16:00. Brief hospitals on expected heat-related admissions.

## Communication
Advisories go to operators first. Public communication is issued only after approval by the communications officer.

## Warm nights
When minimum temperatures stay at or above 30 °C, prioritise outreach to vulnerable groups, including older residents and outdoor workers.
